How to convert PNG to SVG

  1. Add your PNG

    Drag and drop a PNG, paste it from the clipboard, or pick a file. JPG and WebP work too.

  2. Pick a preset

    Logo and Icon keep flat colors and crisp edges, Smooth favors flowing curves, Detailed keeps more colors. Compare the SVG side by side with the original.

  3. Tweak and download

    Recolor, move, resize or delete shapes, then download the optimized SVG or copy its code.

A PNG to vector converter that respects your work

Transparent PNG to SVG

Transparency is preserved exactly. Empty transparent margins are trimmed, and a solid background can be removed in one click.

Real, editable vectors

Your PNG becomes true vector paths, not a bitmap wrapped in an SVG. Edges are traced with sub-pixel precision, corners stay sharp and every color is its own shape, ready for Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape or the web.

Small, optimized files

Before download, the SVG is optimized automatically: paths are simplified and metadata is stripped. You see the file size before and after.

Private by design

Tracing runs locally in a Web Worker on your device. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a PNG to SVG?

Drop your PNG onto the converter, paste it with Ctrl+V, or choose a file. Pick a preset such as Logo or Icon, check the SVG preview next to the original, then click Download SVG or Copy SVG.

Is this PNG to SVG converter free?

Yes. PNGtoSVG is completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no file limits beyond what your device can handle.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion runs entirely in your browser, in a background Web Worker. Your image never leaves your device.

Does it keep PNG transparency?

Yes. Transparent areas stay transparent in the SVG, and empty transparent margins are trimmed automatically so the vector fits your artwork tightly.

Is the SVG a real vector I can edit?

Yes. The PNG is traced into true vector paths, not an embedded bitmap. Each color becomes its own shape that you can recolor, move, resize or delete here, or open in Figma, Illustrator or Inkscape.

Which images convert best?

Logos, icons, illustrations, signatures and other artwork with clear shapes and a limited number of colors. Photos can be vectorized with the Detailed preset, but the result is a stylized, posterized version.
